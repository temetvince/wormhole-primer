## Getting Started

The essential preparations and login habits to get set up safely in wormhole
space before and after joining a corporation.

### Preparation Before Joining

- Before Arrival: Go to a major market hub (preferably Jita or Amarr) and make
  that your home station. You can't respawn inside wormhole space.
- When You Arrive: Use Jump Clones to backup your good clone.
- After You Arrive: Install Jump Clones in major market hubs to maximize
  returnability home if podded.

### What to Bring

- MINIMUM: A ship fit for scanning and exploration (e.g., Covert Ops Frigate,
  Astero, Stratios, or T3 Strategic Cruiser).
- RECOMMENDED: A ship fit for huffing (e.g., Venture or Prospect).
- OPTIONAL: A ship fit for ratting (e.g., T3 Strategic Cruiser, Praxis, Heavy
  Assault Cruiser, or Marauder. Don't forget Salvagers).
- SITUATIONAL: A ship fit for hauling and industry (e.g., DST or a Blockade
  Runner).
- DIPLOMACY: A ship fit for PvP (ask for corporation doctrine).

### When Logging In

- Always join the corporate fleet when logged in. If the fleet does not exist,
  then create it.

## Main Game Loop

The standard daily activities that keep your wormhole operations running
smoothly.

1. Check if home has any new signatures (sigs). If not, move onto the statics
   and do the same thing there.
2. If everything's clear, pick an unscanned wormhole (a leaf in the tree,
   usually) and start scanning that.
3. After you're tired of scanning, you can do the PVE sites you've scanned
   already: gas, relic, and data sites, and some combats if you've got the
   skills for it.
4. Don't forget about your Industry and Planetary Industry jobs/tasks, etc.

## Earning ISK in Wormhole Space

The most reliable and situational ways to make money while living in wormhole
space.

- Not discussed but recommended: Planetary Industry.

### Consistent ISK

- Risk (decreasing): Ratting > Huffing > Mining
- Reward (decreasing): Ratting > Huffing > Mining

#### Huffing

- Optimal Setup: T2 + Faction Prospect or Covetor + boosts in a good cloud can
  yield 100-150m ISK/hr.
- If using a >2600m/s agile Venture or Prospect, try orbiting cloud at 500m with
  MWD on to kite the rats.
  - Huff smaller radius clouds before the rats appear, then transition to the
    larger radius cloud.
  - Vital rats are deadly to this strategy. Avoid unless prepared.

#### Ratting

- C1-2: Inferior and not worth your time if you can do exploration or huffing.
- C3: Up to 200-250m ISK/hr in a Heavy Assault Cruiser; can almost double if
  blitzing in a Marauder (advised against).
- C4: Good money
- C5: Great money if soloing in marauders or running 3 Leshaks/Nestors.

### Inconsistent ISK

- Risk (decreasing): Relic > Data
- Reward (decreasing): Relic > Data

#### Relic/Data Sites

Key strategies and rules for successfully hacking relic and data sites in
wormholes.

Core Node Placement

- The Core node will always be near at least one hostile node.
- Hostile nodes will never spawn in a spot connected to all six surrounding
  spots unless the Core node is one of its neighbors.
- The Core node will be six or more spots away from your spawn unless you spawn
  in a position where every spot is closer than six (even if unconnected). In
  such cases, the Core node can spawn at any random spot, even next to your
  spawn.

General Advice

- Use the wrench item before your virus takes damage to increase health above
  the starting value.
- Save the Shields and Kernel Rot (damage item) for critical situations. It's
  often better to try another path if you encounter a hostile node than to brute
  force through it.
- Use the "Secondary Vectors" item to kill the hostile node that reduces your
  attack power within three turns, during which you can explore other nodes.

## Bookmarking

Opinionated practices for creating and managing bookmarks to stay organized and
safe in wormhole chains.

- Naming Conventions:
  - ".C4s SIG Last3DigitsOfName <4hrs": . = homeward bound; s = static; <4hrs =
    End of Life
- Save Sites: Save under Signatures and wormholes under Wormholes.
- Bookmark Duration: Only save bookmarks for 2 days maximum.
- Wormhole Return: If you go through a hole, bookmark the return.
- Saving Methods: Only save WH by clicking on them from the Overview or in the
  3D view for accuracy. It's okay to save Signatures from the scanner view as
  the loss of accuracy is minimal.
- Before jumping through a bookmarked wormhole with an unknown destination,
  right click the bookmark, and click Edit Location. Leave that window open
  while you jump.

## Wormhole Info

Essential wormhole mechanics, types, indicators, and identification tips.

### General notes

- K162 means the hole was rolled into (at least 1 ship has come through).
- If a static is rolled and no one warps to it, then it will never open.
- If a static is rolled and no one enters it but does warp to it, then it may
  open if <15 hours remaining, and it will open if <4 hours remaining (24 hour
  lifetime).
- C1 and C3 don't have j-space statics and C5+ don't have k-space statics.

### Security Levels

- Unknown: C1/C2/C3
- Dangerous Unknown: C4/C5
- Deadly Unknown: C6
- High Security: Hisec
- Low Security: Lowsec
- Null Security: Nullsec
- Triglavian Space: Pochven

### Wormhole Lifespan

- Less than 1 day remaining (fresh)
- Less than 4 hours remaining (end of life)
- Less than 1 hour remaining
- Expired, closure imminent

### Mass Remaining

- Not Yet: Over 50% (Fresh)
- Not to a Critical Degree: Between 50% and 10% (Shrink)
- Stability Critically Disrupted: Less than 10% (Crit)

### Ship Passage

- Very Large Ships: All ships except for Titans and supercarriers can pass
  through.
- Larger Ships: Battleships, Orcas, and smaller ships can pass through.
- Up to Medium Size Ships: Unplated Nestors, battlecruisers, and smaller ships
  can pass through.
- Only the Smallest Ships: Only frigates, destroyers, or specially fit HICs can
  pass through.

### Viewing Tip for Visual Identification

- Rotate the camera around the wormhole; features aren't always visible from all
  angles.

## Survival Tips

Practical advice to stay alive and avoid common threats while operating in
wormholes.

- Stay Aligned: Unless stationary, always keep your ship aligned to a celestial
  object or safe spot to warp out quickly.
- Watch D-Scan: Continuously use the directional scanner to check for enemy
  ships and probes.
- Use Safe Spots: Create safe spots in systems to hide and observe before
  engaging in activities.
- Cloak When Possible: Use cloaking devices on appropriate ships to avoid
  detection.
- Avoid Predictable Routes: Don't use the same routes repeatedly to reduce the
  chance of ambush.
- Null Sec holes rarely receive traffic.
- Don't PVE in holes with lots of K162 connections.

The main market hubs where you should set up jump clones for easy reshipping and
return trips.

1. Jita IV - Moon 4 - Caldari Navy Assembly Plant: Most popular market hub,
   located in Caldari space.
2. Amarr VIII (Oris) - Emperor Family Academy: Fairly popular, located in Amarr
   space.
3. Dodixie IX - Moon 20 - Federation Navy Assembly Plant: Largest Gallente
   market hub.
4. Rens VI - Moon 8 - Brutor Tribe Treasury: Minmatar hub.
5. Hek VIII - Moon 12 - Boundless Creation Factory: Minmatar hub.

## Roles in the Corp

The main roles players fill in a wormhole corporation.

- Scout: Responsible for scanning and finding wormholes and sites.
- Combat Pilot: Engages in PvE and PvP combat, defending the corp's assets and
  attacking enemies.
- Industrialist: Focuses on mining, manufacturing, and resource management.
- Logistics Pilot: Provides remote repairs and support during fleet operations.
- Tackler: Specializes in pinning down enemy ships during engagements.
- Bubbler: Deploys warp disruption fields to prevent enemy ships from escaping.
- EWar: Disrupts enemy ships by jamming targeting systems, reducing sensor
  strength, and more.

## Warfare Notes

### EWAR

Quickly identify and apply the correct electronic warfare module based on ship
type.

Look at the color of the ship background in the Overview and jam using the
matching colored module:

- Amarr: Radar (Yellow background)
- Caldari: Gravimetric (Blue background)
- Gallente: Magnetometric (Turquoise background)
- Minmatar: Ladar (Red background)

### Tanking

The preferred tank type for each faction.

- Amarr -> Armor
- Caldari -> Shield
- Gallente -> Structure
- Minmatar -> Speed

### Resistance Holes (1st / 2nd)

The primary and secondary resistance holes for different tank types and
factions.

- T1 Shield -> EM / Thermal
- T1 Armor -> Explosive / Kinetic
- Amarr -> Thermal / EM
- Caldari -> EM / Explosive
- Gallente -> Explosive / EM
- Minmatar -> Kinetic / Explosive

### Missiles for Kinetic bonus ships

The best missile types to use on kinetic-bonus ships for different scenarios.

- Scourge Rage - DPS
- Scourge Javelin - Range
- Caldari Navy Scourge - Application
- Mjolnir Rage - EM DPS
- Mjolnir Javelin - EM Range
- Caldari Navy Mjolnir - EM Application

## From HS to NS and Back through Pochven

The specific filaments needed to travel between high-sec, null-sec, and Pochven.

- To NS (quieter systems): Noise-5 'Needlejack' Filament
- To NS (active systems): Signal-5 'Needlejack' Filament
- To Pochven: Border-5 'Pochven' Filament
- To HS (or sometimes LS): Glorification-1 'Devana' Filament
